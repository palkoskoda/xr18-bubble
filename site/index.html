Why I built it

The soundcheck is finished and it is time to play. I need one quick DCA adjustment or a mute, but my phone is on another Wi-Fi: reconnect, open Mixing Station, select the mixer, find the control, then tap. I wanted those essential controls in a small bubble at the edge of the screen, ready from whichever app I was using.

XR18 Bubble grew from that everyday performance workflow. The full mixer app remains one shortcut away; this panel keeps just the controls I choose.

Only what you need

Choose master, DCA, channels, buses or mute groups. The panel fits your selected controls.

Ready to play

Request your saved mixer Wi-Fi, load current values and see live signal meters. Return to your previous Wi-Fi when you finish.

Made for your setup

Light, dark or system appearance. Twelve languages. A configurable shortcut to Mixing Station or another installed app.

Local control, without an account

No ads, analytics or cloud account. Settings stay on your device. Saved Wi-Fi passwords use Android Keystore encryption.

Designed for Behringer XR18/X18 and Midas MR18 over local OSC/UDP. Android 10 or newer. Mixer control traffic uses the hardware protocol’s unencrypted local connection.

Before a performance

Test your phone, mixer and Wi-Fi together. Android may ask for approval when joining a network; automatic switching and return behavior depend on the device.
