# Changelog

## 0.4.2 — independent text size

- Separate Text size on the Controls screen: 75%, 100% (default), 125%, 150%, 175%, 200%, 250% or 300% of the selected control size's text.
- Scales labels, numeric values, mute text, connection status and fader dB markings, while fader handles and meters retain the selected control size. Android system text scaling also applies.
- Panel width and height adapt to larger text, with scrolling when needed; screen-fitted text may be smaller than the requested maximum.

## 0.4.1 — larger controls and drag to stop

- Seven control sizes, from Small through Maximum (4XL), with larger text, fader handles, meters and mute buttons.
- Horizontal and vertical scrolling keeps large controls reachable on smaller screens and with enlarged system text.
- Drag the collapsed bubble onto the bottom × target to stop it; release elsewhere to keep it at the nearest screen edge.
- New size choices and guidance in all twelve app languages. Bubble size retains its three choices.

## 0.4.0 — first public beta

- Configurable app shortcut next to mixer refresh; long press opens a searchable installed-app picker.
- System, light and dark settings themes and twelve offline languages.
- Developer credit and support contact.
- Configurable floating master/DCA/bus/channel/mute-group controls with adaptive sizing and live meters.
- Verified mixer synchronization, remembered mixer Wi-Fi and session-aware return prompts.
- User documentation, screenshots and privacy policy.
