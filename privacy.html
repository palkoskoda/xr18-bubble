Privacy policy

For version 0.4.2 · Last updated 14 October 2026

XR18 Bubble is developed by Pavol Škoda. For privacy questions, contact pavolskoda@azet.sk.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected mixer, its address, Wi-Fi settings, control layout, language and appearance preferences locally. A profile can include a network name (SSID), an optional manually entered access-point identifier (BSSID), and a Wi-Fi password. Passwords are encrypted with a key held in Android Keystore.

After a verified mixer reply, the app can remember the Wi-Fi name and security type carrying that connection. It can also remember the name of your previous Wi-Fi for the return option. It does not read passwords from Android’s saved networks. These settings are never uploaded to the developer.

Permissions and local communication

Android treats reading the Wi-Fi name as location-sensitive. The app may request precise location permission and require Android Location to be enabled for that purpose. It does not read GPS coordinates, determine physical location, scan nearby access points or keep a location history. Nearby Wi-Fi Devices is requested separately on newer Android versions for connection operations.

Android receives the chosen network name and credentials when you request a connection. Mixer discovery, control values and signal meters use local OSC/UDP. The hardware protocol does not encrypt those packets. Protect your local network.

The floating bubble uses Android’s optional display-over-other-apps permission and a user-started foreground service with a visible notification. You can hide the bubble or revoke permissions in Android settings.

App shortcut

The shortcut picker reads apps with a launcher activity installed on your device, displays their labels and icons, and saves the package name you choose. This list and choice stay on your device. Opening the shortcut uses Android’s normal launcher action and does not access another app’s private data.

Collection and sharing

The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, cloud backend or developer-operated crash reporting. The developer does not collect, sell or share your app settings or usage data. The current app has no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

If you contact support, your email is handled to respond to your request. Do not include Wi-Fi passwords or other secrets in support messages.

Retention and deletion

Edit a profile to remove its Wi-Fi details. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes local app data. There is no developer-held copy of your app profile to delete. Android and Google Play manage their own system and installation data under their respective policies.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics, advertising scripts, third-party embeds or cookies added by this project. GitHub Pages provides the hosting. GitHub may process technical information about your visit, including your IP address, under the GitHub privacy statement.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change. Contact Pavol Škoda at pavolskoda@azet.sk with privacy questions.
