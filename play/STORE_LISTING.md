## English

### Short description (80 characters max)

Quick floating controls for a compatible X AIR mixer on local Wi‑Fi.

### Full description (4,000 characters max)

XR18 Bubble puts the mixer controls you use most within quick reach. Open a small floating bubble over another app and adjust saved master, DCA, group, or channel controls without searching through a full mixer view.

Features:

- Select and save a preferred mixer profile.
- Choose the controls shown in the bubble and arrange their labels.
- Connect to a compatible X AIR mixer, including XR18, X18, and MR18 profiles, over the local network using the X AIR control protocol.
- Show connection status and current fader and mute state.
- Keep the selected Wi‑Fi and mixer profile locally on the device.
- Run the optional user-started floating control service with a notification.
- Choose system, light, or dark appearance; the compact floating control stays dark for readability on stage.
- See live signal meters beside the faders while the mixer is connected.
- Use the built-in interface in English, Slovak, Czech, German, Polish, Hungarian, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, or Ukrainian.
- Open a selected installed mixer app from the shortcut area, for example Mixing Station, when it is available on the device.

The app is intended for local operation at rehearsals and live shows. It does not provide cloud storage, user accounts, advertising, analytics, or remote access. Mixer commands are sent over the local UDP connection supported by the mixer; the local network is responsible for network security.

Android may ask for nearby-device Wi‑Fi access, location access used only to read the current Wi‑Fi SSID, notification access, and permission to display over other apps. These permissions support the app's connection, status notification, and floating bubble. Wi‑Fi connection changes may require a system confirmation or a manual action depending on the Android version and device manufacturer.

XR18 Bubble is an independent project and is not affiliated with Behringer, Music Tribe, or Mixing Station. Compatible hardware and network conditions are required. Test the app with your mixer before relying on it during a performance.

Developed by Pavol Škoda.
